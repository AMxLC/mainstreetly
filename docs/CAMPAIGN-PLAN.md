### How AI Tools Actually Find Your Business

AI tools like ChatGPT, Siri, and Google Gemini work the same way Google does: they search the public web. When someone asks ChatGPT to "find a plumber near me," ChatGPT searches the web for plumbers in that area. Your Mainstreetly profile (once indexed) appears in those search results, just like your Google Business page does.

**This is NOT MCP integration. This is public web search—the same mechanism Google uses.**

> When someone asks ChatGPT, Siri, or Gemini to find a plumber near them, those tools search the public web—just like Google does. Your Mainstreetly profile (once indexed) appears in those search results. This is web search, not a special "integration." The same way Google finds your business.

**Q2: Wait—you don't integrate directly with ChatGPT?**
> No. Here's why that matters: ChatGPT doesn't let *most* apps integrate directly. Instead, when ChatGPT needs to find local businesses, it searches the web. The method (web search) is more reliable and works with every AI tool, not just one.

**Q5: What's the real cost?**

## SECTION 9: SUCCESS METRICS & INTERPRETATION

### Primary Metric: Cost Per Lead (CPL)

**Formula:** Total Ad Spend / Total Email Signups

**Benchmarks:**
- **Under $5 CPL:** Excellent. Messaging resonates. Scale this.
- **$5-10 CPL:** Moderate. Viable, but test new creative or targeting before scaling.
- **Over $10 CPL:** Red flag. Messaging isn't resonating. Pause and retool.

### Secondary Metrics

| Metric | Target | Why It Matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | 2%+ | Shows if creative is compelling |
| Conversion Rate (Clicks → Signups) | 30%+ | Shows if landing page converts |
| Cost Per Click (CPC) | $0.20-0.50 | Shows if targeting is efficient |
| Email Open Rate (Email 1) | 40%+ | Shows if subject line works |
| Survey Completion Rate | 60%+ | Shows if ask is low-friction |

### What to Do With Results

**If CPL Under $5:**
- Scale budget 2-3x (next test: $1,000-1,500)
- Test new geographies
- Test new verticals
- Keep creative as-is (don't fix what's not broken)

**If CPL $5-10:**
- Test one variable at a time:
  - New creative (same messaging)
  - Wider age targeting (or narrower)
  - New interests (entrepreneurship vs. business ownership)
  - Different ad copy length (longer vs. shorter)
- Keep budget flat for next test

**If CPL Over $10:**
- Pause current creative
- Test founder credibility angle more (before/after photo)
- Test stat-forward messaging differently (highlight conversion rate, not search volume)
- Test trades-only vertical first (smaller, more targeted)
- Reduce budget to $250 and run Phase 1 again with new creative

## SECTION 11: FOUNDER PLAYBOOK (For Almir)

### What You're Testing

You're not testing whether AI search is real. The data is clear: ChatGPT, Siri, and Gemini are searching for local businesses. You're testing whether **businesses care enough to claim a free spot**.

If CPL is under $5, they do. If it's over $10, they don't—yet. The messaging needs to be sharper.

### What to Watch For

1. **Variant winner (Phase 1):** Which message moved people? Fear (trades)? Founder credibility (all)? Stats (all)? This tells you which angle to double down on in Phase 2 and beyond.

2. **Landing page friction:** If CPL is high but CTR is good, your landing page isn't converting. Make the form even shorter (maybe just email?). Or simplify the copy.

3. **Retargeting signal (Phase 2):** If retargeting CPL is lower than cold traffic, you have a messaging problem. People are interested but not convinced. This is fixable.

4. **Survey completion rate:** Email 1 sends them to a survey. If less than 50% complete it, the survey is too long or unclear. (60 seconds should be fine, but test it yourself.)

5. **Email open rates:** If Email 1 doesn't open well, your subject line is weak. A/B test in the next cycle.

### Founder's Mindset

This is a validation test, not a full campaign. You're looking for a green light (CPL under $5) to scale, or a yellow light (CPL $5-10) to refine.

Don't get attached to any one ad. If it doesn't work, kill it and try another angle.

The best version of this campaign will come from listening to what works—not guessing.

## APPENDIX C: Corrected FAQ for Internal Team

### Q: Why no direct ChatGPT/Siri integration?
A: ChatGPT and Siri don't expose APIs for most apps to integrate directly. Instead, they search the web. When your profile is indexed and SEO-optimized, it shows up in those searches. This is MORE reliable than hoping one platform integrates with you.

### Q: What about MCP (Model Context Protocol)?
A: MCP is a technical standard that only Claude (Anthropic) and Cursor support. ChatGPT doesn't use MCP. So claiming "MCP integration = ChatGPT integration" is false. We use web search instead, which works with every AI tool.

### Q: How is this different from Google Business Profile?
A: Google Business shows you in Google Maps and Google Search. Mainstreetly shows you in ChatGPT, Siri, Gemini, Perplexity—tools that search the web differently than Google. You should use both.

### Q: How do we prevent spam/low-quality businesses?
A: During survey (Email 1 → survey link), we verify business legitimacy. Phone number is optional but encouraged. Once live, we flag abuse and remove non-compliant profiles.

### Q: Why only 5 spots per category per city?
A: Scarcity creates urgency. 5 is small enough to feel exclusive, large enough to be defensible (vs. "one per city," which is unenforceable). It also creates natural pricing tiers later (first 5 get founding pricing, next batch pays more).
